Questions

Do I need to buy a POS terminal?

No. My Store runs on a Windows PC or laptop, or an Android tablet (7 inches or more) as a full cash register. A barcode scanner and a receipt printer work with it, but you can add them whenever you like.

What does it do on a phone?

On an Android phone you can sell, and you can also follow your shop from afar: the sales, profit, debts and the shift's cash recorded on the shop's PC or tablet.

Which languages?

Arabic, French and English. You can switch at any time in the app.

Is my data safe if my PC breaks?

Your shop is backed up to the cloud in your account. On a new PC or tablet, sign in with the same account and your shop comes back, products and pictures included.

How do I pay without a bank card?

When you choose a plan in the app, you can ask to pay without a card, for example by bank transfer or CashPlus. We send you the details and open your plan as soon as the payment arrives.

I need help to start

Watch the tutorials, or write to us on WhatsApp from the Contact page. You talk directly to the person who makes the app.
