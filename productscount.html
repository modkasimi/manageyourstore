Questions

Is productsCount the same app as My Store?

No. productsCount is a separate helper app for your phone. It doesn't sell or print receipts - it prepares your product list and stock count. My Store is the cash register that runs on Windows or a tablet.

Do I need My Store to use productsCount?

No. You can use productsCount on its own, just to count your stock and keep your prices. My Store is only needed if you want to import the list and sell with it.

What if a product's details don't fill in?

Just type the name yourself, once. The app remembers it, so the next time you scan that barcode it's already there.

How do I install it?

Tap Download, choose the free version and enter your email. You receive the Android file (APK) by email - open it on your phone to install. You may need to allow "Install from unknown apps" in your phone's settings.

How do I get the export version?

You don't need to choose it now. Install the free version: the export is open for 15 days, and when you want to keep exporting the app shows you how to buy it - by card, or without a card.
