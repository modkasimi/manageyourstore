Refund Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

We want you to buy only when you are sure My Store fits your shop. That is why it comes with a 15-day free trial, with every feature and no bank card needed. If you still are not satisfied after paying, this policy explains how to get your money back. It covers My Store licenses and subscriptions and the productsCount export unlock.

14-day money-back guarantee

You can ask for a full refund within 14 days of your payment, for any reason, for:

  • a My Store lifetime license;
  • the first payment of a monthly or yearly subscription;
  • the productsCount export unlock.

Subscription renewals

You can cancel a subscription at any time from the app's menu (Unsubscribe), with the link in the email Paddle sends you with each payment, or by emailing us; the app then keeps working until the end of the period already paid, and you are not charged again. Renewal payments are not refunded, unless you ask within 7 days of a renewal you did not intend (for example, you forgot to cancel and have not used the app since), or where the law in your country gives you a right to a refund.

Charged by mistake

If you were charged twice, or charged for something you did not receive, we refund the wrong payment in full, at any time.

How to ask for a refund

Email us at kasimiapps@manageyourstore.site from the email you used to pay (or tell us that email), and say which purchase you want refunded. You do not need to give a reason, but if something did not work, telling us helps us fix it.

What happens next

  • We answer within 2 business days.
  • Card payments are refunded to the same card by Paddle.com, our online reseller and Merchant of Record, which handles returns for all our orders. Your bank may take 5 to 10 business days to show the money back.
  • You can also contact Paddle directly about your order at paddle.net, using the details in your receipt email.
  • Payments made without a card (for example by bank transfer) are refunded by the same method, to the account that paid.
  • When a purchase is refunded, its license is turned off on all devices. Your store data stays on your device.

Contact us
