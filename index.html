Why I made My Store

It hurts me to see shopkeepers who work from morning to night still lost in notebooks and mental arithmetic. They don’t know their real profit, they forget who owes them money, they lose stock without noticing, and they make decisions in the dark. They deserve to see the fruit of their hard work clearly, and to grow.

That is why I made My Store: so simple that anyone can use it, works without internet, in Arabic, French and English, on the PC, tablet or phone you already have, with a one-time price instead of an endless subscription.

— Mohamed, creator of My Store

About Us

Welcome to My Store

We are dedicated to transforming the way small businesses manage their stores. Our journey began with a simple goal: to simplify store management through innovative technology.

We understand the challenges faced by small business owners, from tedious manual processes to the complexities of inventory management. That's why we've developed a user-friendly point of sale (POS) application designed specifically for small shops. Our app combines cutting-edge features like barcode scanning and real-time sales tracking to streamline operations, save time, and boost productivity.

At My Store, we believe that every business deserves access to powerful tools that enhance efficiency and drive growth. Our team is passionate about helping you transition from manual methods to a digital management system that not only simplifies your tasks but also allows you to focus on what you love—serving your customers.

Join us on this journey toward smarter store management, and experience the ease and convenience of modern technology!
