Privacy Policy - My Store

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

This policy covers the My Store app (listed on the Microsoft Store as "my sales") and the free productsCount companion app, both published by kasimiApps, as well as this website (manageyourstore.site). Where their data practices differ, each section says so. It explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

Information We Collect

Account email (My Store). My Store requires sign-in with an email address (via Firebase Authentication), used to identify your account, verify your subscription or trial status, and apply your license across your devices. productsCount has no account/sign-in - it only asks for an email when you check or request a purchase (see below).

Your store data (My Store). Products, sales, purchases, wastage, repairs, customers, suppliers, and cash register entries you record in My Store are stored in a local database on your own device. We do not see or access this data as part of normal operation. productsCount's product/stock-count entries are likewise stored locally on your device only.

Cloud backup, My Store only (optional). If you enable automatic backup in My Store's settings, a copy of your local database is periodically uploaded to our Firebase Cloud Storage, associated with your account, so you can restore it if you reinstall the app or switch devices. This backup is only accessible to your signed-in account. If you never enable backup, your store data never leaves your device. productsCount has no cloud backup feature.

Device identifiers (My Store only). To enforce your subscription's device-activation limit and to show your devices on the in-app "Manage Devices" screen, we generate a device identifier and a readable label (e.g. your computer name or phone model) and send them to our license-management service. We do not use this identifier for advertising or tracking purposes.

Camera / barcode scanning (both apps). If you use a barcode-scanning feature, the camera is used on-device only to read a barcode's numbers. No photos or video are stored or transmitted to us. In productsCount, a scanned barcode number is sent to the public Open Food Facts database to look up and auto-fill product details - see their own privacy policy for how they handle that lookup.

Purchase/unlock checks (productsCount). productsCount's export feature is unlocked per purchase. When you check or request access, we send the email you provide to our server to verify or record that purchase - the app does not otherwise collect an account or sign-in email.

Manual payment requests (both apps). If you choose to request payment without a card, we collect the email, selected plan (My Store) or product (productsCount), and amount (where applicable) you provide so we can arrange payment with you directly.

Website analytics. Pages on manageyourstore.site, including this one, use Google Analytics and the Meta (Facebook) Pixel to understand site traffic and measure ad performance. These use cookies/identifiers in your browser; see Google's and Meta's own privacy policies for details on what they collect.

We do not collect your address, state, province, city, or postal code - the app has no feature that asks for this.

How We Use Information

  • To provide the app's core functionality and keep your account signed in
  • To verify your subscription/trial status and enforce device-activation limits
  • To back up and restore your store data when you enable that feature
  • To respond to support requests and arrange manual payments when requested
  • To understand website traffic and measure the effectiveness of our ads

Third-Party Service Providers

We share data with the following providers only as needed to deliver the service:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud - authentication, cloud storage for backups, and server-side functions
  • Lemon Squeezy - our subscription billing and license-management provider; processes your email and subscription/payment information as the merchant of record
  • Amazon Web Services (SES) - sends transactional emails related to manual payment requests
  • Google Analytics and Meta Pixel - website analytics and ad measurement on manageyourstore.site

Data Retention

Your store data stays on your device unless you enable cloud backup, in which case the latest backup is kept until it's overwritten by a newer one or you ask us to delete it. Manual payment request records are kept for our bookkeeping. Uninstalling the app removes your local data from that device only.

Your Choices

  • You can turn automatic cloud backup on or off at any time in the app's settings.
  • You can ask us to delete your cloud backup and any manual-payment request records by emailing us (below).
  • You can deactivate a device from the "Manage Devices" screen in the app.

Children's Privacy

My Store and productsCount are business tools for shop owners and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent changes.
